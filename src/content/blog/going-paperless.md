Paper-based Food Control Templates make compliance harder than it needs to be. They are easy to misplace, slow to review, and difficult to prepare for an audit.

RRM is being built around a simpler operating model:

- Staff complete daily records from a phone, tablet, or browser.
- Owners and auditors review history from a web dashboard.
- Future IoT and AI-assisted workflows can reduce manual entry even further.

This blog is now powered by Markdown, so product updates, release notes, compliance explainers, and roadmap posts can be added by creating new files in `src/content/blog/`.
