How It Works

A Simple Workflow for Daily Compliance

Set up your checks once, give staff a clear daily routine, and keep management visibility without extra admin.

01

Set Up Your Site

Create your restaurant profile, load your Food Control Templates, and assign the right checks to the right team members.

02

Complete Checks Each Shift

Staff complete food safety and operational checks on mobile throughout the day, with a simple workflow that is easy to follow.

03

Review Gaps and Follow Up

Managers can quickly see what has been completed, what is overdue, and where follow-up is needed before small issues become bigger problems.

04

Share Records When Needed

When an inspection or internal review is due, export a complete, timestamped report in seconds and share it with confidence.
